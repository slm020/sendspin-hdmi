Player & audio

HDMI outputs are named after the connected TV or receiver once it's detected. The device the player is using can be missing from a normal scan. Rescan stops the player so every device shows.
If this player is out of sync with your other speakers, adjust the delay. Music Assistant also has a per-player delay setting (Settings → Players → this player).

Screen

1080p is safest. On some Intel mini PCs, 4K gave no picture and also cut HDMI audio.
Lyric timing: positive shows lines later, negative earlier.

Groups

Saved members of a Music Assistant sync group play whenever that group starts. If this player restarts partway through a song, Music Assistant leaves it out until the group starts again. Auto-rejoin puts it back.
Loading groups
So that taking the player out of a group by hand later on is respected.

Amp control

Run something when playback starts and stops, e.g. switch your AV receiver on and to the right input. Announcements count as playback too.
The stop action is cancelled if music starts again within this time, so pauses and track changes don't switch the amp off.
